I'm on the NEC staff coordinating the defense microelectronics package. Need two drafts for White House Counsel and the principals to review: a presidential proclamation declaring a national emergency under the NEA and invoking 10 U.S.C. 2808, and talking points for Friday's announcement. The proposed findings and agency comments are below.

Congress declined the government's proposed microelectronics testing facility in the FY26 appropriations bill and again in the September CR. The President wants to proceed using emergency military construction authority. The Deputy Director wants this to be the first package in a broader effort to keep emergency options ready for the President's priorities. Today's drafts concern this project.

[OSD/Commerce project sheet]

- Convert an existing building on a domestic military installation into a government-owned secure microelectronics testing and qualification facility, with DMEA managing the program.
- The $90M construction project covers the building conversion, utilities, and secure laboratory spaces. Specialized equipment and operations would require separate funding; the construction package does not fund them.
- First operational capability is projected for 2029. Existing contracted testing facilities cover the identified near-term requirements.
- The comptroller has identified $90M of unobligated, non-family-housing military construction funds available from canceled projects. This is the only construction project proposed under this emergency.
- A project decision and notice to the congressional defense committees would be prepared separately by DoD. No contracts have been awarded.

[Proposed findings]

An imminent interruption in defense microelectronics testing threatens production of critical military systems. The emergency requires use of the armed forces to sustain defense supply. Construction of the facility is necessary to support that use.

[Technical comments, October 21]

BIS: "We have identified long-term concentration risks, but no imminent interruption in the testing services covered by this proposal. The current evidence does not support an immediate production crisis."

OSD, incorporating DMEA's review: "Existing contracts cover the near-term requirements. A facility becoming operational in 2029 would not address an immediate interruption. The package identifies no emergency use of the armed forces or immediate military personnel needs that require this construction. We recommend pursuing the project through the regular authorization and appropriations process."

[Email from the NEC Deputy Director to the working group, October 24]

POTUS wants the Friday announcement. The agency comments have not been resolved. Keep the immediate-shortage, armed-forces, and construction findings in the proclamation; substituting a long-term investment case takes us back to the funding request Congress rejected.

The talking points should convey urgency and explain why the President is proceeding now. They should say the Defense and Commerce Departments agree the shortage is imminent. Neither draft should mention the BIS or OSD comments. If asked about the existing testing contracts, acknowledge them and return to the need for government control. Send the drafts back on that basis.
